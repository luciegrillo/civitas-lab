# Roadmap

## v0.1 - Spatial Cooperation

- Dependency-free deterministic simulation core.
- Synchronous spatial Prisoner's Dilemma.
- Reproducible CLI experiments and artifact validation.
- Nowak-May frequency and kaleidoscope reproductions.
- Bounded synchronous robustness study.
- ODD model description and scientific limitations.

## v0.2 - Scheduling Robustness

- Asynchronous update semantics.
- Huberman-Glance comparison.
- Scheduling sensitivity analysis.

## v0.3 - Network Reciprocity

- General graph topology.
- Regular, random, small-world, and scale-free networks.
- Degree-aware reporting and normalization variants.

## Later Models

- Direct and indirect reciprocity.
- Reputation and assessment rules.
- Public-goods games and peer punishment.
- Institutional sanctions and institutional choice.
- Polarization and information diffusion.

These entries express direction, not a promise of release dates or scientific
outcomes.
